# Customer Intake Portal

> **New here? Open [`START-HERE.md`](./START-HERE.md) first.** It walks you through publishing this to the internet in about 30 minutes, using ChatGPT as your guide.

A beautifully designed inquiry portal for your business. Deploy your own copy in a few minutes — the finished app lives in your GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase accounts. You own it forever.

Part of **[The Owner Toolkit](https://theownertoolkit.co)** — a growing collection of professionally designed business software that owners can deploy and own.

## What you get

- A polished setup wizard to customize your portal
- A customer-facing inquiry form with your logo, brand color, and welcome message
- An owner dashboard with a drag-and-drop pipeline (New → Contacted → Quoted → Booked → Won → Lost)
- Every submission versioned against the exact form the customer saw
- File uploads with a secure signed-URL flow
- Private owner notes on every inquiry

## Ownership

This is not a hosted service. Your portal runs in **your** Vercel project, backed by **your** Supabase database, in **your** GitHub repository.

- If you cancel The Owner Toolkit newsletter, your portal keeps working.
- Your customer data stays in your Supabase project. Nobody at The Owner Toolkit sees it.
- Update, extend, or replace anything. It's your code.
