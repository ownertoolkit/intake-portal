# Start Here

You just downloaded your **Customer Intake Portal**, customized for your business. Everything you chose in the wizard — your business name, logo, brand color, welcome message, and questions — is already inside this folder. You do not need to configure any of that again.

This document is the only one you need to read before starting.

## What this is

A private, branded intake form for your business. Customers visit a link you send them, fill out the form you designed, and you see their inquiries in a dashboard only you can access.

Once it's live, you'll move inquiries through a pipeline: **New → Contacted → Quoted → Booked → Won → Lost.**

## What you'll do next — about 30 minutes

You'll use **ChatGPT** as your installer. It will guide you through every step. You won't need to touch code.

You'll create **two free accounts** along the way (three if you want the code version-controlled — ChatGPT will explain):

| Service | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Vercel** | Runs your portal on the internet | Free for typical small-business use |
| **Supabase** | Stores your customer inquiries and uploaded files privately | Free for typical small-business use |
| **GitHub** *(optional)* | Keeps a version-controlled copy of your code | Free |

**All accounts are yours.** Your business owns everything from here on out. If you cancel your Owner Toolkit newsletter subscription, your portal keeps working.

## How to start

1. Open **[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)** in your web browser. A free ChatGPT account is fine.
2. Start a new conversation.
3. **Drag this entire folder** into the ChatGPT message box to upload it. ChatGPT can read folders.
4. Open the file called **`INSTALL-WITH-CHATGPT.md`** in this folder. Select everything inside it, copy it, and paste it into ChatGPT. Send.
5. ChatGPT will take it from there and walk you through every step, one at a time.

## If ChatGPT ever gets confusing

Tell it:

- *"Please explain that more simply."*
- *"I'm not sure what to click. Can you describe the button?"*
- *"Is there an easier way?"*

You can send it a screenshot of what you're seeing if you're stuck. It can read images.

## If you get truly stuck

Reply to the email that sent you this and we'll help. Please include:

- What step you were on
- What ChatGPT said last
- What you tried

## When you're done

You'll have:

- A live customer intake portal at a URL you can share with your customers
- A private dashboard where you see and manage every inquiry that comes in
- Full ownership of everything — the code, the database, the files, the customer information

Nothing goes through The Owner Toolkit's servers. Your customer information is yours.

Enjoy.
