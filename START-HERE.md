# Start Here

You just downloaded your **Customer Intake Portal**. This is the only file you need to read before starting.

## What this is

A private, branded intake form for your business. Customers visit a link you send them, fill out a form you designed, and you see their inquiries in a beautiful dashboard.

You'll customize:

- Your business name and logo
- Your brand color
- The welcome message customers see
- The questions you want to ask

Once it's live, you'll move inquiries through a pipeline: **New → Contacted → Quoted → Booked → Won → Lost.**

## What you'll do next — about 30 minutes

You'll use **ChatGPT** as your installer. It will guide you through every step. You won't need to touch code.

You'll create **three free accounts** along the way:

| Service | What it does | Why you need it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GitHub** | Stores your portal's code | It's where your portal lives before it runs anywhere — like a digital filing cabinet | Free |
| **Vercel** | Runs your portal on the internet | It's the "landlord" that gives your portal a public web address | Free for typical small-business use |
| **Supabase** | Stores your customer inquiries and files | It's the locked filing cabinet where inquiries and uploads live | Free for typical small-business use |

**All three are free to start.** If your portal gets very heavy use (tens of thousands of monthly visits or hundreds of megabytes of file uploads), one or both may eventually cost $10–25/month. You'll never hit those limits while getting started.

**You will never pay The Owner Toolkit for hosting.** All three accounts belong to you. If you cancel your newsletter subscription, your portal keeps working.

## How to start

1. Open **[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)** in your web browser. A free ChatGPT account is fine to use.
2. Start a new conversation.
3. **Drag this entire folder** into the ChatGPT message box to upload it. (Yes, ChatGPT can read folders.)
4. Open the file called **`INSTALL-WITH-CHATGPT.md`** in this folder. Select all of the text inside, copy it, and paste it into ChatGPT. Then send.
5. ChatGPT will take it from there and walk you through every step, one at a time.

## If ChatGPT ever gets confusing

You can always tell it:

- *"Please explain that more simply."*
- *"I'm not sure what to click. Can you describe the button?"*
- *"Is there an easier way?"*

Send it a screenshot of what you're seeing if you're stuck. It can read images.

## If you get truly stuck

Reply to the email that sent you this and we'll help. Please include:

- What step you were on

- What you tried
